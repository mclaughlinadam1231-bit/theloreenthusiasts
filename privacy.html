Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy covers the private publishing tool used by The Lore Enthusiasts (the "tool"). The tool is used only by the channel's owner to manage the owner's own accounts.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube (via the YouTube Data API): uploading the owner's videos, setting their titles, descriptions, settings and thumbnails, and reading their processing status.
  • Instagram (via the Instagram API): publishing the owner's Reels with captions and cover images.
  • Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts): the owner's own data, read and edited at the owner's request.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to carry out the owner's requests. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The tool does not collect data about any other person.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google/YouTube) and in Instagram's settings under Apps and websites.

Contact

Questions: message @theloreenthusiasts on Instagram.
